## Brand Identity & Faithful Recreation
1. **Preserved Original Visual Identity:** Recreated the official Photo Phactory visual language, minimalist monochrome contrast (`#000000` / `#FFFFFF`), and olive green accent (`#6DA716` / `#275304`).
2. **Key Feature Modules:**
   - **Revolution Slider 6:** Full-viewport hero slider featuring 3 rotating fine-art photography collections with animated progress bars, navigation arrows, and quick tag pills.
   - **Milestones Counter:** Preserved 4 key achievements (10+ Years Experience, 250+ Clients, 350+ Projects, 5+ Industry Awards).
   - **Thin. Light. Epic Quote Section:** "Start Something New" brand philosophy module with background imagery and call-to-action button.
   - **Complete Portfolio:** 24 fine-art portfolio compositions across Wedding, Pre-Wedding, Newborn, and Portrait with filter tabs and full-screen image lightbox modal.
   - **Real Wedding Stories:** Deep-dive photo essays with couple narrative, venue details (Samode Palace, Goa, Udaipur, Rishikesh), highlight quotes, and photo galleries.
   - **Cinematic Video Showcase:** Video teasers and cinematic wedding films with duration badges, play buttons, and interactive video modal.
   - **Client Testimonials:** Full-width testimonial carousel with genuine quotes from couples and families (Rakhi Sehgal, Dhruv Mehta, Ray Fernandes).
   - **About Us & Founder Story:** Dedicated company overview celebrating lead storytellers Vishal & Bunty, candid methodology, and worldwide destination coverage.
   - **Photography Journal & Blog:** Articles covering bridal preparation, pre-wedding destination guides, and candid shooting philosophies with full article read modal.
   - **Consultation & Booking Modal:** Multi-field booking inquiry form with date selector, event type, location, and guest count.
   - **Floating Quick Actions:** Persistent WhatsApp direct chat and direct phone dialer.
