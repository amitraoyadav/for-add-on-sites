## 1. Overview & Architecture

PK Photography is a luxury wedding cinematography and portrait studio operating across Mumbai, Goa, and international destination wedding venues. This template reproduces the complete look, feel, typography, interactive galleries, and conversion funnels of the flagship site.

### Key Features
- **Hero & Cinematic Reel**: Headline, brand narrative statement, and mobile 4K wedding video reel with playback modal (`/assets/pk-photography/wedding-reel-mobile.mp4`).
- **4-Step Narrative Workflow**: Gather, Feel, Remember, Keep.
- **Client Logos Directory**: 32 verified enterprise and luxury brands (Tata Motors, Siemens Energy, Godrej, Myntra, Airbnb, AMD, W Goa, Asianet, Lakme, BenQ, etc.) with category filters.
- **Visual Folio Highlights**: 8-frame curated editorial photography gallery with high-resolution lightbox viewer showing camera optics and lighting specs.
- **Studio Packages**: Transparent pricing from ₹6,000 (Executive Headshots) to ₹2,00,000 (Multi-Day Destination Wedding).
- **Client Testimonials**: Authentic 5.0★ Google ratings and verified client quotes.
- **Frequently Asked Questions**: 7 verbatim accordions covering pricing, delivery timelines, Goa travel logistics, and private gallery sharing.
- **Inquiry & Availability Modal**: Direct date reservation form.
- **Concierge Channels**: Direct WhatsApp (+91 8888766739) and studio telephone connectivity.
