1. **Isolated & Zero-Pollution Design:** Self-contained React component with internal state-based sub-navigation (`home`, `stories`, `portfolio`, `services`, `booth`, `about`, `pricing`, `contact`).
2. **Hero Section with Multi-Slide Storytelling:** Dynamic crossfade hero slider featuring authentic editorial headlines ("MUMBAI · INDIA - Stories Worth Remembering. Moments Worth Reliving."), verified Google 4.9★ rating badge (500+ reviews), and quick action triggers.
3. **Six Featured Real Wedding Stories:** Deep-dive photo stories including Moheet ❤️ Prerna (Royal Destination), Anuj ❤️ Sayali (Traditional Maharashtrian), Sayali ❤️ Raghvendra (Beachside Sunset), Apoorv & Bhakti, Jinal & Divya Shah, and Gaurav & Sneha with full image galleries and narrative context.
4. **Interactive Filterable Portfolio:** Multi-category selector (`All`, `Weddings`, `Pre-Wedding`, `Maternity & Baby`, `Corporate & Events`, `Food & Product`) with full-screen Lightbox viewer.
5. **Dedicated Premium Instant Photo Booth Showcase:** Mumbai's leading live photo booth service featuring 8-second lab-quality prints, QR code phone saving, custom frame branding, and complete booth package pricing.
6. **"From a Smartphone to Gajare Photography" Founder Story:** The authentic journey starting with a ₹20,000 loan, 8+ years of craft, 650+ celebrations, and 500+ verified 5-star Google reviews.
7. **Transparent FAQ & Investment Guidance:** Detailed breakdown of Essential Moments, Complete Celebration (Popular), Photo Booth add-on, and an in-depth comparison of Candid vs Traditional photography.
8. **Comprehensive Booking & Date Availability Flow:** Interactive modal with date selector, event type picker, budget estimator, services checklist, and instant WhatsApp integration (`+91 7208659598`).
