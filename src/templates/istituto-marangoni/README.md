## Institutional Identity & Accreditations
- Founded in Milan in 1935 by Giulio Marangoni.
- Legally recognized by the Italian Ministry of University and Research (MUR) under the AFAM system (Alta Formazione Artistica, Musicale e Coreutica) awarding First Level Academic Diplomas (180 ECTS, Bachelor's equivalent).
- Validated degrees in partnership with Manchester Metropolitan University (MMU, UK) and RNCP Level 7 accreditation (France).
- Licensed by the Knowledge and Human Development Authority (KHDA) in Dubai and Florida Department of Education (FL-DOE) in Miami.
- Ranked in the QS World University Rankings Top 100 for Art & Design worldwide.
